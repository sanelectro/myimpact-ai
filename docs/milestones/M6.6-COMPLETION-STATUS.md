### Current reviewer journey

1. Open an assigned case and enter or revise the label and rationale.
2. Click **Submit review** below that case. This saves an editable entry and
   moves to the next unsaved case, or the dashboard after the last case.
3. Reopen any saved, non-final case to edit it.
4. Once all assigned cases are saved, the review dashboard shows
   **Submit final review**.
5. Open the full preview; use **Edit review** for any case and return after
   saving the change.
6. Click **Confirm and submit final review** to finalize all the current
   reviewer's primary entries together. Only then are those entries locked.

Post-AI opinions retain their separate per-case flow after primary final
submission and remain linked to a persisted AI attempt.
