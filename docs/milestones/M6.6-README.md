# MyImpact — M6.6 Impact Analysis, 1:1 and Career Report

## Milestone overview

**Milestone:** M6.6  
**Status:** Planned; acceptance criteria and implementation are not confirmed complete.

M6.6 gives employees an evidence-grounded view of progress against goals and expectations, helps them identify useful next contributions, prepares them for monthly manager 1:1 discussions, and creates reviewable career reports.

## Impact analysis

- The initial analysis period is monthly. Quarterly and yearly analysis views may follow after the monthly model is validated.
- Compare employee goals against explicit expectations available from their goals, role/company context and manager. Preserve each expectation's source; flag missing or conflicting expectations instead of inventing them.
- Represent expectations as goal criteria with equal weights by default. Employee/manager-adjusted weights may be supported.
- Goal and milestone scores use only evidence submitted by the employee and accepted by their reporting manager. Unreviewed or rejected evidence does not contribute.
- AI may propose evidence-to-criterion matches and evidence-based partial progress, with source links and confidence. The employee confirms or corrects a proposed partial value.
- Explain scores, evidence coverage, gaps and uncertainty. Recommend next contributions tied to unmet criteria, clearly distinguished from completed work.
- Use the same evidence eligibility and scoring rules for goal and milestone scores.

## User workflows

### Monthly impact and 1:1 preparation

1. The employee reviews the month's expectations and progress, including criteria, score, supporting evidence and confidence.
2. The employee submits evidence; their reporting manager accepts or rejects it. Rejected or unreviewed evidence is excluded from scores.
3. The employee reviews AI-proposed evidence matches and partial criterion progress, then confirms or corrects the proposed progress.
4. The employee creates and edits a monthly 1:1 brief with progress, completed goals, milestone status, evidence awaiting review, blockers, gaps, suggested next contributions and discussion topics.
5. The employee can save and revisit a private draft and add private notes/questions.

### Career report

- Start with a quarterly impact/career summary; custom reporting periods may be supported by the underlying model.
- Generate a structured, reviewable draft from completed goals, in-period goal progress, manager-accepted evidence and impact assessments.
- Include period boundaries, generation date, source dates and traceable references for factual claims.
- Let the employee review, correct, remove or add content before any user-initiated print/export.

## Access and trust boundaries

- Analysis and reports are private to their owner by default.
- Reporting managers receive only the minimum access needed to review and accept/reject evidence submitted by their reports. This does not grant access to private 1:1 notes or career reports.
- Do not add organization-wide reporting, automatic manager delivery, performance ratings, calibration or predictive promotion recommendations in this milestone.
- Keep AI suggestions, source facts and user-authored text distinct. Unsupported claims must be omitted or flagged.
- Creating or editing a report must not rewrite evidence, goal state or assessments.
- Keep source references and show when saved content is stale after underlying goals or evidence change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Expectations and criteria show their source; missing or conflicting expectations are visible.
- [ ] Criterion weights default to equal weighting; any supported weight changes are validated and shown.
- [ ] Only employee-submitted, manager-accepted evidence contributes to goal and milestone scores.
- [ ] Manager review is limited to the employee's evidence and enforced by the API; acceptance/rejection and resubmission behavior is defined and tested.
- [ ] AI-proposed evidence matches and partial progress are traceable, include confidence, and require employee confirmation/correction before the proposed partial value is used.
- [ ] Score calculation is consistent across goal, milestone, report and dashboard surfaces; migration from the legacy M6.3 evidence-count model is defined.
- [ ] Monthly analysis explains progress, evidence coverage, gaps and uncertainty without unsupported claims.
- [ ] Suggested contributions are clearly labeled and never treated as completed work.
- [ ] The 1:1 brief is employee-facing, editable and private; notes are not exposed through manager evidence review.
- [ ] Career-report periods and source dates are explicit; edited drafts can be saved and revisited without changing source records.
- [ ] Empty, incomplete, stale and out-of-period data are handled explicitly.
- [ ] Owner-scoped access protects analysis and reports; reporting-manager access is limited to evidence review.
- [ ] API/service tests cover evidence approval, scoring, periods, source traceability and access boundaries; UI and end-to-end tests cover evidence review, monthly analysis, 1:1 preparation and career reports.

## Decisions to resolve before implementation

- Define evidence-review states, rejection reasons, resubmission, audit history and notification behavior.
- Define how expectations from goals, role/company context and managers are reconciled or deduplicated.
- Define supported adjusted-weight permissions and validation.
- Define how a manager's acceptance of evidence and an employee's confirmation of AI-proposed partial progress are represented independently.
- Decide report version retention and first-release export format.
