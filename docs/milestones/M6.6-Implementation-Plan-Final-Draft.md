## 1. Purpose and precedence

Implement Goal Intelligence and Goal Management while preserving Goal identity, historical commitments, evidence provenance and existing functionality.

This draft consolidates the latest discussion, including the accepted recommendation to freeze history permanently, explicitly replan eligible ongoing/future work, and reference completed or frozen work without rewriting its commitment.

Sources and context:

- [Blocker clarifications](../../../z-myimpact-rough/blockers.txt): the six-decision working document. Some statements were subsequently superseded in discussion.
- [Earlier Phase 1 assessment](M6.6-Goal-Management-Phase-1-Assessment.md): useful source inventory, but not the final product-policy authority.
- Later conversation decisions: recursive permanent descoping, continuation handover, and exceptions to parent-driven replanning for completed/frozen work.

Where earlier assessments conflict with this draft, the consolidated policies below take precedence for subsequent planning. The earlier assessment is retained unchanged as historical analysis.

### Superseded approaches

- Do not create independent Goal revision records or clone a whole hierarchy whenever an ongoing Goal definition changes.
- Do not introduce Goal Families merely to version an existing Goal.
- Do not require additional formal manager approval to publish an employee-confirmed revision of an existing ongoing commitment.
- Do not automatically inherit evidence approval across changed Goal definitions.
- Do not leave descendants executable when their ancestor is recursively descoped.
- Do not provide reinstatement of permanently descoped Goals.
- Do not force revised parent dates onto completed or frozen historical commitments.
- Do not implement Jira MCP in this milestone; use read-only Jira REST.

## 3. Confirmed product contract

### 3.1 Identity, ownership and ordinary creation

- Every Goal is employee-owned, including team/organization-scoped Goals.
- Normal creation produces a new Goal ID in Draft and follows the normal approval/activation lifecycle.
- Material changes to an existing eligible ongoing commitment retain its Goal ID and produce an immutable GoalVersion.
- Continuations and replacements receive new Goal IDs.
- Similar titles do not establish identity; matching recommendations require review.

### 3.2 Employee-confirmed definition publication

For an existing ongoing Goal:

1. Employee reviews the proposed definition and affected context.
2. Employee supplies a material-change reason.
3. Employee explicitly confirms discussion/alignment with the manager.
4. An explicit publication action creates/selects the new immutable operative definition.
5. Prior definitions, operative periods and historical results remain accessible.

No additional manager Goal-definition approval is required for this publication. The confirmation is an employee attestation, not a recorded manager approval.

Phase 0 confirmed that semantic definition edits require versioning/publication, while unchanged values and whitespace-only corrections do not. Publication is immediate on success with server-authored UTC audit time, without backdating or scheduling initially. Deterministic checks and persisted READY validation for the exact proposed definition are required; validation is not formal manager approval.

Autosave, source ingestion, LLM output and proposal generation are not publication. Preparing a proposal must not modify current execution.

Definition publication does not automatically change the Goal's lifecycle status. Ordinary initial approval and activation requirements remain applicable. Existing latest-version approval checks must be reconciled with this revised-definition policy; they must not recreate the manager/approval gate that this decision removed.

### 3.3 Recursive permanent descoping

- Descoping affects the selected Goal and every structural descendant.
- Each affected Goal becomes permanently read-only while retaining its lifecycle status.
- Preserve definitions, evidence captures, decisions, assessments, relationships and history.
- Exclude the frozen subtree from current execution and assessment.
- Keep it visible with a clear Descoped indicator.
- No reinstatement endpoint, action or UI workflow.
- Fresh work uses new Draft Goals, optionally linked to historical work.
- Continuation/replacement lineage is not a structural child edge and must not expand the recursive descoping boundary.
- Prevent structural edits, new descendants, execution updates, lifecycle transitions and ordinary deletion that would bypass permanent preservation.

### 3.4 Continuation of unfinished work

- Do not move the original Goal into the next quarter.
- Create a new Draft Goal for remaining work under the appropriate next-quarter planning context.
- Preserve the original Goal ID, definition, lifecycle and historical records.
- Creating the continuation freezes its predecessor for further definition/execution updates.
- Future progress, evidence and assessments for the carried scope belong to the latest continuation.
- Do not automatically mark the predecessor Completed, Archived or Descoped.
- Completed subgoals are not continued.
- Preserve immediate predecessor, original root, rollover iteration and reason as lineage.
- Use a display suffix such as `-continued (1)`. Numbering is per lineage/rollover, not the number of sibling Goals.
- Do not copy evidence approvals, assessment results or completion state.

The predecessor freezes at continuation creation, even while the new Goal is Draft. The UI must disclose that execution may pause until normal approval/activation completes. Do not silently move the freeze point to activation.

The remaining ambiguity about lifecycle/relationship actions on a continuation predecessor is a deferred contract item, not permission to mutate it without restriction.

### 3.5 Completed-Goal replacement

- Do not rewrite or reopen a Completed Goal merely to introduce a materially different commitment.
- Preserve it as Completed and mark it descoped, retaining its original achievement.
- Create a new independent Draft Goal and record replacement lineage.
- Do not automatically copy criteria, evidence, approvals or results.
- Optional historical evidence use follows explicit selection and approval.
- Apply recursive descoping consistently if the replaced Completed Goal has structural descendants.
- UI wording must distinguish superseded commitment from failed/cancelled achievement.

### 3.6 Parent definition/date changes

- Publish a new immutable parent definition, retaining the parent Goal ID.
- Review affected ongoing/future children explicitly; do not clone them.
- Retain child IDs and unchanged definitions wherever compatible.
- Associate eligible current planning context with the new operative parent version.
- Preserve prior parent-version context for historical interpretation.
- Completed and frozen Goals are excluded from mandatory date changes; they remain historical references without rewritten commitments.
- Do not automatically rewrite descriptions, criteria or milestones.
- Detect date/milestone conflicts and block publication until the selected current plan is valid.
- Validate containment at every relevant hierarchy level, not just Annual-to-descendant containment.
- Historical attribution is not current execution eligibility or automatic achievement credit.

Recommended date behavior for detailed contract design:

- Keep dates unchanged when they already fit an expanded parent period.
- Preview explicit changes when a parent period narrows or moves.
- Never silently clamp, proportionally compress or invent child dates.
- Preserve actual execution/completion dates; missing actual dates remain unknown.
- Require explicit resolution when an ongoing/future child has no valid planning overlap.

### 3.7 Evidence and historical achievements

- Employee selects historical evidence for a specific target Goal definition.
- Reuse starts pending manager reapproval.
- Only approved applicable evidence contributes to the revised definition's assessment.
- Preserve the original association, approval and historical result regardless of reuse outcome.
- Bind review to exact evidence content, association meaning and target definition.
- Historical achievement resubmission is another entry point to this same independent evidence-review process, not a second evidence engine.
- Reuse physical/source content where appropriate; "submit again" does not require duplicate Evidence entities or duplicate document storage.
- Do not transfer achievement scores or claim that old work demonstrates newly introduced commitments automatically.
- New definitions without assessed/approved support display an honest unassessed/pending state, not inherited progress or an invented zero score.

## 4. Cross-cutting implementation invariants

1. Explicit operative definition is never inferred solely from highest snapshot number.
2. Historical reads use their recorded definition, criterion, milestone, evidence and review context.
3. Candidate preparation does not invalidate current execution or historical results.
4. Retained lifecycle status alone never establishes execution eligibility.
5. All mutation paths enforce ownership and frozen-state rules, including legacy APIs and background jobs.
6. Every multi-Goal command is all-or-nothing; no hidden helper commits/rollbacks.
7. External retrieval and LLM work occur outside mutation transactions.
8. Revalidate exact reviewed revisions and the final selected graph at publication.
9. Stale input produces a visible conflict, not a silent rebase.
10. Idempotent retries return the original command result without duplicating Goals or repeating publication.
11. Recursive operations use complete authoritative membership, never a truncated UI page.
12. Shared Evidence remains independently governed; freezing one Goal does not globally freeze/delete the source item.
13. Audit actor derives from authenticated identity, not employee-editable source text or LLM output.
14. Ordinary archival/deletion routes cannot erase history promised by these rules.

### Phase 2 - Permanent recursive descoping

**Work**

- Implement explicit reason/confirmation, affected-tree preview and atomic recursive freeze.
- Preserve lifecycle outcomes and show frozen disposition separately.
- Enforce restrictions across Goal, criteria, milestones, evidence, lifecycle, hierarchy, delete and job paths.

- Keep continuation/replacement lineage outside structural recursion.

**Tests**

- Freeze affects all descendants, not only the first page or traversal depth.
- Concurrent child creation/reparenting cannot escape the freeze.
- Existing descendants cannot be detached or new children attached to bypass read-only behavior.
- Shared evidence remains usable elsewhere; historical capture does not change.
- All legacy and background writes reject forbidden mutations.
- No reinstatement route/action exists; ordinary deletion cannot erase retained history.

### Phase 3 - Evidence reuse and assessment isolation

### Phase 8 - Goal Management reconciliation and selective apply

**Work**

- Build a bounded orchestrator over the preceding domain commands, not a parallel Goal engine.
- Match source observations to existing Goals and propose add/update/replan/retain/descope/continue/replace/no-change/clarification as appropriate.
- Resolve operation dependencies and show complete selected impact.
- Persist reviewed decisions with source and expected-version context.
- Revalidate a selectively accepted final plan; merge compatible same-Goal operations or reject conflicts.
- Apply all accepted operations atomically and idempotently.
- Publishing an ongoing-definition change requires the employee's explicit reason/attestation; plan acceptance alone must not silently supply it.
- New Goals remain Draft. AI and source events never approve, activate, complete or achieve Goals.

**Tests**

- Frozen/Completed Goals route to the correct reject/new-work/replacement path.
- One failed selected operation rolls back the entire apply.
- Stale plans require review; retries do not silently substitute current revisions.
- Prompt injection, unsupported tool action and cross-employee references are rejected.
- No planning path calls achievement/impact scoring or lifecycle autoapproval.

**Exit:** reviewed selective application is correct end-to-end across document and Jira sources.
