## How to read this catalog

The expected results and category labels in this document are provisional reference labels. They are intended to guide dual human review and must not be treated as approved ground truth or release thresholds. The original 14 cases remain unchanged as the historical exploratory baseline.

Every case includes role context and an evaluation period in the executable dataset. The assessment prompt receives those fields, but the AI must still rely on the supplied criterion and evidence rather than inventing missing context.

## Governance and execution notes

- The benchmark is a new dataset version and does not overwrite `m66-intelligence-comparison-v1`.
- All labels are provisional until two independent reviewers complete review and adjudication.
- Citation grounding uses `UNGROUNDED` as the provisional negative label.
- Correctness and robustness results should report numerator and denominator, but remain `NOT_EVALUABLE` until the reference labels and threshold policy are approved.
- Running the benchmark produces up to 102 persisted AI attempts; it does not itself approve the model or thresholds.
- The benchmark should be executed through the Admin Reliability page as a separate experiment using dataset ID and version `m66-intelligence-comparison-v2`.
