# Lead Software Engineer

## Role focus

Provide hands-on technical leadership and guide a team to deliver high-quality
software aligned with business goals.

## Responsibilities

- Shape scalable, efficient, maintainable designs with the architect and guide
  implementation and engineering standards.
- Tackle complex technical problems and model high-quality code and review
  practices.
- Mentor engineers across experience levels and communicate technical
  decisions to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Break work into manageable tasks, help prioritize and plan delivery, and
  identify technical, capacity, and schedule risks early.
- Improve delivery processes, testing, reliability, and feedback practices.
- Connect team technical work to business priorities and consider cost alongside
  technical quality.
- Balance hands-on engineering with team leadership responsibilities.

## Suggested starter goals

- **Improve team technical direction and quality:** Guide a design or
  engineering-practice improvement with appropriate architectural alignment.
- **Strengthen predictable team delivery:** Improve planning, work breakdown,
  prioritization, or risk management for team commitments.
- **Develop engineers and align work to outcomes:** Mentor teammates and connect
  technical decisions to stakeholder needs and business goals.
