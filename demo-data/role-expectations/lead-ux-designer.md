# Lead UX Designer

## Role focus

Set a user-centered design direction across Fleet products while balancing
hands-on design work, cross-functional collaboration, and design-team
leadership.

## Responsibilities

- Advocate for user needs and align design direction with business goals.
- Design complex workflows through wireframes, prototypes, and detailed designs;
  validate and refine them with research and usability testing.
- Build and maintain design systems that support consistency and scalability.
- Collaborate with product managers, developers, and stakeholders, clearly
  communicating design rationale.
- Mentor designers, lead design reviews, and align team work to product vision
  and measurable user value.
- Apply relevant UX practices and improve design workflows and quality.
- Deliver intuitive, accessible experiences and balance immediate needs with
  longer-term design strategy.

## Suggested starter goals

- **Improve a user-centered product experience:** Validate and refine a complex
  workflow using user research or usability testing.
- **Strengthen design-system consistency and accessibility:** Improve reusable
  design-system guidance and apply it across relevant product experiences.
- **Grow design practice and measurable user value:** Mentor designers, lead
  collaborative reviews, and connect design outcomes to user needs.
