# Role catalogue, responsibilities, and competencies

This framework summarizes job-family descriptions, role titles, and
skill-level matrices alongside the role profiles in this directory.

The competency matrices are a reference, not a complete set of individual role
descriptions or goal targets. A blank competency rating means no level is
specified; it does not mean the competency is unnecessary. Confirm role naming,
expectations, and proficiency with the relevant manager before using this
framework for performance decisions.

## Job-family responsibilities

### Software engineering

Design, develop, test, deliver, maintain, and improve business applications as
part of a team across the software lifecycle. Engineers may work across the
stack or specialize in areas such as frontend or backend development, using
efficient code, suitable frameworks, and established patterns.

The engineering matrix adds capabilities in programming, application design,
requirements modeling, cloud and platform technologies, data stores,
DevSecOps, Agile methods, automated testing, security, monitoring, performance,
source control, documentation, domain knowledge, collaboration, mentoring, and
technical roadmaps.

### Data science

Turn data into actionable insights using statistics, AI, and machine learning.
Work with stakeholders to translate business problems into analytical
questions, gather and integrate relevant data, develop appropriate solutions,
and explain their capabilities and limitations. Solution quality, including
prediction accuracy, is a responsibility.

### User experience and design

Help business and IT teams understand user, business, and stakeholder needs;
prototype early; design intuitive interfaces for internal and external users;
verify usability; and participate in reviews of relevant deliverables.

### Solution architecture

Design products and solutions for value streams within enterprise-architecture
guardrails. Advise on architectural issues and options, inspect and optimize
current services, define target setups, and support planning and communication
around service operation and outsourcing.

### Engineering management

Manage and provide technical direction to multiple Scrum teams. Own quality
assurance for code and features, work with the wider organization to remove
impediments, and coach teams and product owners in Agile practices, user
stories, and backlog management.

### Scrum mastery

Establish Scrum practice and improve Scrum Team effectiveness. Coach teams and
the organization on Agile values and empirical ways of working, support
effective Scrum events and impediment resolution, help address conflict, and
build stakeholder understanding of Agile practices.

### Technical program delivery

The catalogue names business analysts, project managers, Scrum Masters,
program and portfolio managers, and program directors across levels. Detailed
responsibility statements are not available for most of these roles, so this
summary does not infer role-specific duties for them.

## Relationship to the role summaries and goal seed

- The seven role profiles include detailed responsibilities. The role
  catalogue provides competency context for overlapping roles; use both
  sections together.
- This framework broadens the catalogue to additional role families, but it
  does not create individual Markdown files or goals for every listed title.
  Roles without detailed responsibilities need manager review before getting
  role-specific goal templates.
- The SQL seed remains limited to the seven detailed role profiles.
  Competency ratings describe proficiency levels, not employee-specific
  outcomes, measurable targets, or agreed goals; converting them directly into
  seeded goals would invent expectations.
