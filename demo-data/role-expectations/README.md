# Role expectations

These role summaries and the accompanying [role catalogue and competency
framework](role-catalogue-and-competencies.md) are starter references, not a
formal performance framework. Role expectations can vary by team; employees and
managers should review and tailor goals before approving them.

The role-specific goal suggestions in these files are seeded as **draft personal
goals** by `myimpact-db/scripts/demo/seed_role_goals.sql`. The seed matches
`users.role` to the documented title (case-insensitively) and skips roles that
are not covered by a detailed role profile. Competency ratings are not
converted into goals because they do not define employee-specific outcomes or
target thresholds. The seed does not assign performance ratings or make
promotion decisions.
