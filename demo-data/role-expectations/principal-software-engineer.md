# Principal Software Engineer

## Role focus

Extend technical leadership beyond one team by influencing strategic
architecture, long-term technical direction, and engineering culture across
multiple teams.

## Responsibilities

- Guide high-level architecture across projects and develop a technology vision
  aligned with long-term business goals.
- Partner with product, engineering leaders, architects, and executives to
  shape technical strategy and organizational priorities.
- Address complex, cross-team technical challenges and manage risks to system
  stability, security, performance, scalability, and maintainability.
- Drive innovation and promote engineering practices such as code quality,
  testing, CI/CD, and DevOps across teams.
- Mentor senior engineers and emerging technical leaders; influence engineering
  culture without relying on direct people management.
- Contribute to long-term roadmaps and represent engineering excellence within
  and, where appropriate, outside the organization.

## Suggested starter goals

- **Set cross-project technical direction:** Guide an architectural or technology
  decision across projects and communicate its alignment with long-term goals.
- **Resolve a cross-team technical challenge:** Coordinate a sustainable
  response to a complex issue affecting multiple teams or products.
- **Scale engineering practices and technical leadership:** Strengthen a shared
  engineering practice and mentor senior engineers or emerging technical
  leaders.
