# Senior Software Engineer

## Role focus

Apply deep technical judgment and autonomy to complex work while strengthening
the capabilities and delivery of the wider team.

## Responsibilities

- Design scalable, secure, and robust systems; make considered technical
  decisions and collaborate with architects.
- Own substantial work through delivery, balancing trade-offs such as
  performance, scalability, security, and simplicity.
- Review code and provide constructive feedback; mentor junior and mid-level
  engineers and lead technical discussions.
- Contribute to planning and sustainable delivery with engineering managers,
  product, and other partners.
- Improve development processes, quality, testing, performance, and reliability.
- Document designs and communicate technical topics clearly to stakeholders.
- Align technical contributions with business goals and the product's direction.

## Suggested starter goals

- **Lead robust technical design:** Shape a system or feature design with
  documented trade-offs and appropriate architectural collaboration.
- **Own a substantial delivery end to end:** Plan and deliver a multi-week
  technical initiative with appropriate quality and stakeholder communication.
- **Grow engineering capability through mentorship:** Provide actionable review
  feedback and support teammates' technical development.
